The Raine Study

To access phenotypic, genetic, or biological data from The Raine Study cohorts, researchers must utilize the centralised online submission framework. Direct coordination should be maintained with the science team during planning phases.

  • Data Discovery: Comprehensive longitudinal pregnancy and birth cohort study, with over 35 years of longitudinal data. It primarily focuses on Generation 2 (Gen2), the original Raine Study babies, with data collected from prenatal to adult life, covering physical and mental health, development, lifestyle, genetics and environment. Explore Data & Biosamples.
  • Application Pathway: Submit and manage project proposals via the formal Raine Online Submission System (ROSS) Portal.
  • Mandatory Prerequisites: A completed Data Management Plan (DMP) draft along with clear Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) approval verification.
  • Cost & Curation Framework: Curation models depend heavily on institutional Unincorporated Joint Venture (UJV) partnership status. Biosample access scales from a base baseline fee. Detailed schedules can be verified at the Raine Project Costing Flowchart.
