WA Cohort Data Access

Information for Researchers: Access to these datasets is managed via the governance workflows of the respective custodians. Before initiating an application, please review the specific cohort tools, catalogues, and pricing matrices on each cohort page.

Each cohort page covers the same four topics: Data Discovery, Application Pathway, Mandatory Prerequisites, and Cost & Curation Framework.

  • The Raine Study

    Longitudinal pregnancy and birth cohort with over 35 years of data, from prenatal to adult life, covering physical and mental health, development, lifestyle, genetics and environment.

    Applications via the Raine Online Submission System (ROSS)

    Explore Study →

  • The ORIGINS Project

    Databank tracking maternal, paternal, and multi-year childhood timelines.

    Applications via the ORIGINS REDCap Expression of Interest

    Explore Study →

  • Busselton Health Study

    Unit-record data collected since 1966, maintained on behalf of the Busselton Population Medical Research Institute (BPMRI) by the University of Western Australia.

    Applications via the BPMRI Database Access Form

    Explore Study →
