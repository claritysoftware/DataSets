The ORIGINS Project

The ORIGINS Project Databank hosts a deep repository tracking maternal, paternal, and multi-year childhood timelines. Sub-projects are required to independently secure and demonstrate the capacity to meet comprehensive infrastructure and processing expenditures.

  • Data Discovery: Interrogate the active databank, participant metrics, and longitudinal tracking variables via the live ORIGINS Power BI Search Catalogue.
  • Application Pathway: Project tracking must be initiated via the official ORIGINS REDCap Expression of Interest (EOI) Entry Point.
  • Mandatory Prerequisites: Information to be added.
  • Cost & Curation Framework: Standard fee schedules evaluate active versus non-active data extractions alongside questionnaire modules (such as the Australian Eating Survey or Early Conners matrix). Project estimates can be evaluated using the ORIGINS Interactive Cost Estimator.
