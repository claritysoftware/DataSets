Busselton Health Study

Busselton Health Survey data collected since 1966 is maintained on behalf of the Busselton Population Medical Research Institute (BPMRI) by the School of Population and Global Health at the University of Western Australia (UWA).

  • Data Discovery: Research activities undertaken as part of the Busselton Health Studies. Data Summaries.
  • Application Pathway: Download and complete the formal physical submission document through the BPMRI Database Access Form.
  • Mandatory Prerequisites:
    • The investigational layout must formally feature an integrated, active BPMRI-affiliated co-investigator.
    • Signed conditions verifying that newly generated or derived calculations and data elements will be returned to the core UWA database repository upon project conclusion.
  • Cost & Curation Framework: Fees comprise a two-tiered structure split between programming costs (extracted on full-cost recovery from UWA) and a foundational maintenance contribution paid directly to the institute.

Studies under the Busselton Health Study

The Busselton Healthy Ageing (Baby Boomer) Study follows a single cohort of Busselton adults born between 1946 and 1964. The cohort was first assessed in Phase 1 (BHAS) and has since been reassessed in Phase 2 (BBBS) and Phase 3 (BHS24). Each phase is a separate dataset with its own data dictionary. The application pathway, prerequisites, costs and contacts on this page apply to all three.

  • Phase 1: Busselton Healthy Ageing Study BHAS

    Baseline assessment of the Healthy Ageing cohort: 5,107 adults born 1946–1964, covering health, lifestyle and demographic measures.

    Data collection
    2010–2015 (completed)
    Participants

    Availability

    Second assessment of the BHAS cohort, following up the same participants to track changes in health as they age.
